## Groups and special terms — optional

- 寅申巳亥: a group associated with movement imagery, not automatic individual 驛馬.
- 子午卯酉: a group associated with interaction, attraction or display imagery, not automatic individual 桃花.
- Personal 神煞 require an explicit reference (such as 年支 or 日支) and lookup rule. The supplied source does not provide a complete personal lookup; leave it unresolved rather than importing one.
- Local special-column list: **戊辰、庚辰、丙戌、壬戌**, named `魁罡四合` in this convention. Record the matched pillar position; applicability and effects require their own rule. Do not replace this list with the common 魁罡 list or import its usual interpretations.
- 半祿 is the local name for 陽干羊刃. Root grade stays in the root skill; it does not mean half-strength or half a share.
