# 取象與核實

Source: the review document, sections 三、七–十、十九. Move from positioned symbols to action, then possible domains and questions that can check them. This stage does not create missing action conditions.

## Inputs

Use the user's question, supplied real-life context, and positioned structural results from [mangpai-work](../mangpai-work/SKILL.md), including uncertainties and any scoped timing results. A vocabulary-only question may use [symbolic-vocabulary.md](references/symbolic-vocabulary.md) directly; label it as vocabulary, not a reading of an unseen chart.

## Procedure

1. **Select the supported structure.** Name the actors, relationship, support / obstruction, connection to 主位 and time scope. Retain whether it is a candidate or a conditionally supported path.
2. **Choose a limited domain.** Use the question and [symbolic-vocabulary.md](references/symbolic-vocabulary.md) to propose plausible functions. For example, a candidate 食傷生財 path can suggest connecting output to resources; it cannot uniquely identify a trade, employer or income.
3. **Keep alternatives.** Offer the few interpretations most relevant to the question and show what distinguishes them. Do not list every possible life event. An unresolved upstream condition keeps the interpretation conditional.
4. **Check against supplied life facts.** Mark facts as user-provided and interpretations as hypotheses. Ask a concrete, neutral verification question when useful. Do not treat agreement with a broad statement as proof of the doctrine or fill unknown biography with assumed events.
5. **Deliver the bounded result.** Explain the rule and concrete path before the possible interpretation. State what it cannot yet select or determine; do not use generic uncertainty language in place of identifying the missing condition.

## Domain rules

- Keep 正財／偏財 as 陰陽 classifications. They do not determine lawful income, gambling, fortune size or stability of employment.
- Keep 比劫、傷官、官殺、偏印 as roles, not moral or personality verdicts. 比劫 can have both support and competition candidates; 偏印 alone is not 梟奪食.
- For 六親, state the chosen mapping before applying it. The source uses 印 for maternal support, 偏財 for paternal resources, 比劫 for peers; 男命財／女命官殺 for partners and 男命官殺／女命食傷 for children. If the needed mapping input or convention is absent, keep this branch unresolved and continue non-kinship interpretation. Do not infer a person's identity from a star.
- Star counts do not count partners, marriages or children, and 正偏 does not establish infidelity. The mapping does not prove real relationship quality.
- Traditional body symbolism is vocabulary, not a diagnosis, disease forecast or basis for treatment. If asked for health interpretation, keep the symbolic statement distinct from medical facts; do not invent a condition.
- Branch groups and special names need their own specified lookup basis. Do not declare individual 驛馬／桃花 from group membership alone. A special-column list match does not import unrelated 魁罡 meanings.
- An opening, clash or 墓 phase does not determine wealth, catastrophe, death or the exact date of a real event. Avoid fear, moral blame and inevitable-fate phrasing.
