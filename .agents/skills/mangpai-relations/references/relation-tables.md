## 沖、穿、刑、破

| Type | Accepted combinations | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 六沖 | 子午、丑未、寅申、卯酉、辰戌、巳亥 | Clash and functional damage are separate. |
| 六穿／六害 | 子未、丑午、寅巳、卯辰、申亥、酉戌 | Same lookup pairs under these two names. |
| 原局三刑 | 寅巳申、丑未戌 | All three original branches required. |
| 破 | 子卯、卯午 | This local list is not the common 六破 table. |

子卯刑 and 辰午酉亥自刑 are background classifications mentioned by the source, not adopted rules for this workflow. Do not add them silently. For 寅巳申, retain the specific 穿、合、沖 relations instead of attributing every effect to 三刑.
