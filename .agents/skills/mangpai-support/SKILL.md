# 虛實、真假與旺衰

Source: the review document, sections 十三–十四. Preserve three distinct questions: local support, whole-chart root eligibility, and the adopted strength conditions. None alone proves effective 做功.

## Inputs and prerequisites

Accept positioned pillars plus the full original root inventory from [mangpai-roots](../mangpai-roots/SKILL.md); obtain it if missing. Root roles, when relevant, come from [mangpai-binzhu](../mangpai-binzhu/SKILL.md). For a rule-only or partial question, use the supplied facts and keep unknown scope explicit.

Evaluate 虛實／真假 separately for each of the four exposed original stems. Evaluate 旺衰 for the day master by default, or for explicitly named participants. Do not add hidden stems as new exposed subjects. Reuse [foundations.md](../mangpai-analysis/references/foundations.md) for element relationships.

## 1. Record support facts before labels

For each subject, record:

- Qualified root in its own branch, and qualified roots elsewhere. Preserve excluded 長生 and 土 exclusions.
- Own branch's 本行, relevant hidden stems, and candidate generation/same-element support.
- Any explicitly adopted 生助／同黨 predicate: 本行 only, specified 藏干, or demonstrated effective action. Record its scope; never silently choose one.
- Support under that predicate as `有／無／未定`, with participants and reasons. An excluded root may still participate in a different relation, but cannot regain root status through the support test.

When the predicate is missing, list the distinct candidate channels and what they could establish. Apply labels already entailed by roots; keep support-dependent labels conditional. Do not treat a possible hidden-stem supporter as confirmed effective assistance. Do not turn absence of a supplied predicate into absence of support.

## 2. Judge local 虛實 and whole-chart 真假

| Conditions within the original chart | Local 虛實 | Whole-chart 真假 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualified root in the same pillar | 實／坐實 | 真 |
| No local root; confirmed local 生助; root elsewhere | 實（坐下有助） | 真 |
| No local root and confirmed no local 生助; root elsewhere | 坐虛／虛透 | 真 |
| No root anywhere; confirmed local 生助 | 實（坐下有助） | 未定（U10） |
| No root anywhere; confirmed no local 生助 | 坐虛／虛透 | 假 |
| No local root; local 生助 unresolved | 虛實未定 | 真 if a qualified original root exists; otherwise 未定 |

Complete four-branch coverage is necessary to claim `全局無根`. If coverage is incomplete, report known positive roots but do not assert a global absence. 虛透 and 真 can coexist. These terms describe the stated scopes, not honesty, usefulness, or personality.

## 3. Judge 旺衰 under an explicit predicate

For the named subject, let M＝月令助、H＝時支助、E＝月干／時干／日支至少一處同黨. M, H and E are support propositions, not counts of qualified roots. List the actual positions that make them true or false under the adopted predicate.

| M / H / E | Result under the source convention |
| --- | --- |
| M=true and H=true | 旺 |
| Exactly one of M/H=true, the other=false, and E=true | 旺 |
| M=false and H=false | 弱 |
| Exactly one of M/H=true and E=false | 未定（U09） |
| Required support facts unresolved | 未定; show any conditional result supported by the known facts. |

Do not infer M/H from root presence alone. 月令 50% and 時支 45% are incomplete conventional weights, not an implementable percentage score; do not fill the remaining 5%, average them, or invent a cutoff. 旺點 labels do not rerank roots or break 主根 ties.

If the user supplies a narrower support predicate, apply it and label the assumption. If the supplied predicate leaves action/impairment unresolved, distinguish its lookup result from effective support. Preserve environmental observations separately; 燥濕 does not rewrite the 土 root table.

## 4. Keep semantic shifts separate

`假官當財／過河財` changes a proposed resource interpretation, not 正官／七殺 identity. The general applicability and exclusions are unresolved (U11). Do not infer it solely from no qualified root, no exposed 財, or an undefined local-support result. Preserve the original 十神 and the conditional reason if discussed; pass interpretation to [mangpai-work](../mangpai-work/SKILL.md).
